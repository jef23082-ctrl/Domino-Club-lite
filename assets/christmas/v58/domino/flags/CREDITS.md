## Socle et hampe

`stand.webp` est issu d’Imagegen avec la référence fournie par le club. Consigne utilisée

> Use case: product-mockup, isolated PNG game asset. Use the supplied screenshot as visual reference for ONLY the desktop flag stand at the left of the three panels. Reconstruct its empty freestanding gold flagpole and base as a photoreal, front camera studio cutout: tall perfectly straight slim warm polished brass pole, fluted decorative collar and spearhead finial at the top, round stepped dark emerald marble plinth with engraved gold ornamental flourishes and polished layered brass rims, column foot mechanically attached to plinth. Match the exact amber highlights and table camera angle of the reference. Entire silhouette fully visible including spear tip and bottom of the base, vertical axis exactly centered, base diameter about 28 percent of the pole height as in the screenshot. Tight 2:3 portrait crop around this single object, empty transparent margin on all sides, true alpha backdrop. NO flag, no cloth, no pennant, no lettering, no floor, no table, no other objects. This is an alpha layer for a separate simulated fabric mounted on the pole.
